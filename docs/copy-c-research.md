# Copy C research and editorial brief
Reviewed October 27, 2026. C is a distinct copy direction, not a finding that it converts better. No traffic experiment or user study has been run.

## Research questions
What does CCC actually offer? Which local questions should a West Florida owner raise early? What can ICF copy responsibly explain? How can review navigation make comparisons predictable?

| Primary source | Finding used | Editorial decision |

## Three copy positions
A preserves existing company wording.
B refreshes the original intent with a warmer, more direct 2026 West Florida voice.
C helps visitors work through decisions: property, intended use, construction method, relevant experience and the next conversation.

## C content architecture
Residential: life and property first; new build, addition or renovation; ask about applicable local reviews.
Commercial: operations, site and stakeholders; connect the requirements before selecting the approach.
ICF: explain the wall, the complete envelope and the need for project-specific design.
Process: discover, develop and deliver, framed as a discussion rather than a guaranteed schedule.
Resources: original capability PDF plus official local and building-science links.

## Boundaries
Copy is marketing content, not engineering, permitting or legal advice. Do not invent prices, completion dates, savings, certifications, awards or named case-study results. Original logo, photography and capability PDF are retained. B and C have their own URLs; A remains available. All versions remain review concepts with email-draft inquiry forms.
